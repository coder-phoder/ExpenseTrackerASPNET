EXPENSE TRACKER - FEATURE ROADMAP
=================================

Difficulty guide
  LOW     Small change inside existing pages. No new tables, packages or
          outside services.
  MEDIUM  New table + migration, a new page, a NuGet package, or sending email.
  HIGH    Background jobs, outside integrations, new infrastructure, or
          security-critical flows.

Within each level, items are listed in priority order: build top to bottom.


ALREADY BUILT
-------------
  - Landing page, register, login, logout (cookie login, hashed passwords)
  - Dashboard
  - Expenses page: add, edit, delete and list (title, price in Rs, date,
    payment mode, optional description)
  - Calendar: month navigation; click a day to see and manage its expenses
    in pop-ups
  - Analytics: summary cards, monthly and weekday charts, payment-mode split,
    largest expenses
  - Profile page
  - Every user sees and changes only their own data

LOW DIFFICULTY
--------------
 1. Logout in the navbar
    Logout is only on the dashboard today; show it next to Profile on every
    page.

 2. Success messages
    "Expense added / updated / deleted" banner after each action.

 3. Total on the expenses page
    Sum of the listed expenses shown under the table.

 4. Search, sort and filter the expenses list
    Search by title, sort by date or price, filter by date range and payment
    mode.

 5. Dashboard summary
    This month's spend, the last 5 expenses and a quick "Add expense" button.

 6. Day totals on the calendar
    Each day's total shown inside its cell, so busy days stand out without
    clicking.

 7. More payment modes
    UPI, debit card, credit card and net banking alongside cash.

 8. Edit profile
    Change your name; change your email with a duplicate check.

 9. Remember me
    Optional "keep me logged in" checkbox on the login page.

10. Return to the requested page after login
    Open the page the user was trying to reach (same-site links only)
    instead of always the dashboard.

11. Friendly 404 and error pages
    A styled "page not found" page and a production error page with no
    developer details.

12. Dark mode
    Light/dark theme switch, remembered per browser.


MEDIUM DIFFICULTY
-----------------
 1. Categories
    A category on every expense (Food, Travel, Bills, Shopping, ...) plus
    user-defined ones; filter by category; spend-by-category chart.

 2. Monthly budgets
    Overall and per-category budgets with a progress bar and warnings at 80%
    and 100%.
    Builds on: Categories.

 3. Change password
    Current password, new password and confirmation.

 4. Income and balance
    Record income; show the monthly balance and savings rate.

 5. Pagination
    Page through long expense lists while keeping search, sort and filters.

 6. Export
    Download expenses as CSV/Excel, and a monthly statement as PDF.

 7. Import from CSV
    Upload a file, preview and validate the rows, then save; skip duplicates.

 8. Receipt attachments
    Attach a photo or PDF to an expense, with file type and size checks.

 9. Forgot password
    Emailed, time-limited password reset link.
    Needs: an email service.

10. Email verification
    Confirm the email address when registering.
    Needs: an email service.

11. Login protection
    Lock an account after repeated failed logins; limit how often /Login and
    /Register can be called.

12. Sign in with Google
    Google login, linked to the account with the same verified email.

13. Delete account and download my data
    Export everything, then permanently remove the user and their expenses.

14. Analytics filters and comparisons
    Date-range filter, this month vs last month, year over year, and a
    category breakdown.

15. Automated tests
    Integration tests for login, expense add/edit/delete, and per-user data
    isolation.


HIGH DIFFICULTY
---------------
 1. Recurring expenses
    Rent, subscriptions and EMIs created automatically each period; pause,
    edit or stop a series.
    Needs: a background job.

 2. Reminders and notifications
    Bill reminders, budget alerts and weekly summaries by email or push.

 3. Shared groups and bill splitting
    Invite people to a group, split expenses equally or by share, track who
    owes whom, and settle up.

 4. Two-factor authentication
    Authenticator-app codes plus one-time recovery codes.

 5. Bank / UPI statement import
    Turn statement exports into expenses, with duplicate detection and
    automatic categories.

 6. Receipt scanning (OCR)
    Read the amount, date and merchant from a receipt photo to pre-fill the
    form.

 7. Smart insights
    Month-end spending forecast, alerts for unusual expenses, category
    suggestions.

 8. Installable app with offline entry (PWA)
    Add expenses without a connection and sync when back online.

 9. REST API
    Token-secured API with documentation, for a mobile app or integrations.

10. Admin panel
    User roles; manage and disable accounts; usage statistics.

11. Production deployment
    Docker, a CI/CD pipeline that runs tests and migrations, HTTPS, secrets
    kept out of the code, database backups, logging and monitoring.


SUGGESTED ORDER
---------------
  Do First -> Low 1-6 -> Medium 1-3 -> Medium 15 (tests) -> the rest by
  priority.
